Standard list of general interests
==================================

The goal of this specification is to specify a standard list of general high-level interests and topics.

Notes:
- There may be some overlap between them. Overlaps are ok, as multiple tags can be applied to an object.
- This is not meant to list every possible specific topic possible, but rather a short high-level list. In other words, this is short and generic on purpose. If more specificity is required, clients and relays MAY employ other techniques to further filter and narrow down content.

## List of interests

- "bitcoin": Bitcoin-related topics (e.g. Bitcoin, Lightning, e-cash etc)
- "technology": Any non-Bitcoin technology-related topic (e.g. Linux, new releases, software development, supersonic flight, etc)
- "science": Any science-related topic (e.g. astronomy, biology, physics, etc)
- "lifestyle": Lifestyle topics (e.g. Worldschooling, Digital nomading, vagabonding, homesteading, digital minimalism, life hacks, etc)
- "travel": Travel-related topics (e.g. Information about locations to visit, travel logs, etc)
- "art": Any art-related topic (e.g. poetry, painting, sculpting, photography, etc)
- "health": Topics focused on improving human health (e.g. advances in medicine, exercising, nutrition, meditation, sleep, etc)
- "music": Any music-related topic (e.g. Bands, fan pages, instruments, classical music theory, etc)
- "food": Any topic related to food (e.g. Cooking, recipes, meal planning, nutrition)
- "sports": Any topic related to sports (e.g. Athlete fan pages, general sports information, sports news, sports equipment, etc)
- "religion-spirituality": Any topic related to religion, spirituality, or faith (e.g. Christianity, Judaism, Buddhism, Islamism, Hinduism, Taoism, general meditation practice, etc)
- "humanities": General humanities topics (e.g. philosophy, sociology, culture, etc)
- "politics": General topics about politics
- "other": Other miscellaneous topics that do not fit in any of the previous items of the list
